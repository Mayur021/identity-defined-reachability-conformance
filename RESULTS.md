`Self-reported` records whether the runner and the implementer are the same party.
`Re-runnable` records whether a third party can reproduce the run from what was
published. `Run origin` records where it was executed. Those three columns are what
separate evidence from an assertion, and an entry missing them is not a claim.

`P1 PASS · P2 PASS · P3 PASS · P4 NOT_ESTABLISHED · P5 PASS · P6 PASS_FOR_ENUMERATED_SURFACE`.
